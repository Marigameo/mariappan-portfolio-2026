Palm leaves, drawn closer to the real thing

Only the hero drawing changed from round 03. It now reads as an olai bundle: leaves stacked under a written top leaf, a red cord knotted in the middle with a beaded tassel, a wooden board beneath, the iron stylus (ezhuthani), a clay lamp and marigolds. The cards and copy are as before.

The top leaf carries Thirukkural 391, கற்க கசடறக் கற்பவை கற்றபின் நிற்க அதற்குத் தக: learn what’s worth learning, thoroughly, then live by it. It felt right for a page of skills, and it’s easy to swap.
