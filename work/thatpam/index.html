The idea

Talent shouldn't be gated by the language a course happens to be taught in. தட்பம் set out to teach front-end craft in Tamil, live, in small cohorts, by someone who actually ships it for a living.

What I owned

  • Brand, landing page and course design, in Figma and in code.
  • The learning experience: cohort structure, live sessions, assignments and feedback loops.
  • Payments, onboarding emails and the boring-but-essential operations around them.

What stayed with me

Cohorts are paused, but the learnings are permanent.

Running everything solo is the fastest way to learn which parts of a product people actually feel. More detailed notes are on the way.
