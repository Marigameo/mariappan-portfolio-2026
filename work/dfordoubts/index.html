DforDoubts

A live e-learning platform that helps learners find experts, and answers. Taken from an idea on a napkin to pixels to production, solo.

Role
Founder, designer, full-stack engineer
Stack
React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, WhatsApp API
Status
Live
Link
dfordoubts.com

The idea

Every learner has a doubt that never made it to the teacher. DforDoubts started as a simple promise: put a real expert one message away, then grow into a place where highly personalised courses are shaped by the people who actually work in the field.

What I owned

  • Brand, marketing site and the course catalogue, from Figma to shipped code.
  • The learner journey: search, free demos, sign-up, live sessions and the "Ask your doubts" loop on WhatsApp.
  • Educator onboarding, payments, GST-aware pricing and the operations behind live classes.

How it works

Learners search by course or instructor, sit through a free demo, then join a live cohort in the language they are most comfortable in. Educators get a profile, a schedule and a payout, and nothing else to worry about.
